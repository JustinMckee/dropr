# Product Overview

## Philosophy

Build a trusted marketplace for niche hobbyist communities. Focus on curated, event-based drops that create anticipation and discovery. Serve makers, modders, and collectors who value quality over quantity. Enable P2P transactions with escrow protection while maintaining the excitement of limited releases.

## Product Description

dropr is a curated, event-based marketplace connecting indie brands and trusted curators with engaged hobbyist communities through limited mystery drops.

**Phase 1 (MVP):** Time-limited product drops—curated events featuring limited-run items, themed bundles, and surplus from verified makers.

**Phase 2 (Subscriptions):** Recurring maker subscriptions sold by month or quarter (aligned with "time to make" and "time to mod"), with defined durations (e.g., 3-month subscription, 3-quarter/9-month subscription). This allows curators to build predictable revenue and buyers to receive regular curated shipments.

## Target Communities

The platform serves three culture clusters:

- The Mod Collective: Mechanical keyboards, PC modding, gaming setups
- The Make Collective: DIY electronics, 3D printing, modular synth builders
- The Mini Collective: Miniatures, model kits, figurine collectors

## Key User Types

- Curators: Create and manage drops (hobbyists, power curators, indie brands)
- Buyers: Enthusiast consumers seeking curated bundles and limited editions

## Core Value Propositions

- Trust and escrow protection for P2P transactions
- Event-based discovery and anticipation
- Limited runs, handmade gear, exclusive releases, themed curation by renowned community members
- Standardized tier pricing and value expectations
- Community-driven reputation systems
- Niche audience targeting for indie brands
