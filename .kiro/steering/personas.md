# Personas

## Philosophy

Design for real people with real needs. Personas represent our target users and guide feature prioritization and UX decisions. When building features, always ask: "Which persona needs this?" and "Does this solve their pain points?" User-centered design starts with understanding who we're building for.

## Persona Overview

Use these personas to guide user-centered design and feature development. When building features, consider which persona(s) will use them and ensure the implementation addresses their specific goals and pain points.

## Culture Clusters

Personas are grouped into three culture clusters, each representing a distinct community of interest. These clusters help us understand the unique needs, motivations, and pain points of our target users.

### The Mod Collective (Mod, Moder)

**What:** _Mechanical Keyboards + PC Modding + Gaming Setups_

The Mod (Moddifier) Collective is a community of enthusiasts who share a passion for customizing and modding their gear, particularly in the realms of mechanical keyboards, PC modding, and gaming setups. The community showcases their projects, exchanges ideas, and collaborates on modding endeavors. The collective fosters creativity and innovation within the modding community, offering resources, tutorials, and a supportive environment for both beginners and experienced modders alike.

### The Make Collective (Make, Maker, DIYer)

**What:** _DIY Electronics + 3D Printing + Modular Synth Builders_

The Make Collective is a vibrant community of makers and DIY enthusiasts who are passionate about electronics, 3D printing, and modular synthesizer building. The community shares knowledge, projects, and resources related to these creative pursuits. Enthusiasts collaborate on innovative projects, exchange ideas, and provide support to one another in their maker journeys. Whether you're a seasoned maker or just starting out, it's a welcoming community that helps one explore and grow their skills in the world of DIY electronics and modular synthesis.

### The Hobby Collective (Hobby, Hobbyist, Miniatureist, Modeler)

**What:** _Miniatures + Model Kits + Figurine Collectors_

The Mini Collective is a passionate community of hobbyists who share a love for miniatures, model kits, and figurine collecting. The community showcases their collections, exchanges tips and techniques, and collaborates on projects related to miniature painting, modeling, and collecting. Enthusiasts connect across different online platforms, learn from each other, and celebrate their shared passion for the art of miniatures. The community helps one explore and grow thier love for tabletop miniatures and model kits.

## Curators

Curators are the heart of our marketplace. They create and manage drops, curate interesting bundles, and engage with buyers. They can range from hobbyists looking to monetize surplus parts, semi-professionals treating this as a side income, to indie brands launching limited runs.

<br/>

### 🧑‍🔧 The Hobbyist Curator (Supply & Culture Seeder)

|**_👤 Profile_**|
|-  
|Deeply involved in one niche (e.g., mechanical keyboards, PC modding, modular synth DIY)  
|Has surplus components from upgrades  
|Active in Reddit/Discord communities  
|Not a business owner

|**_🎯 Goals_**
|-
|Monetize surplus cleanly
|Build reputation inside community
|Avoid informal P2P friction

|**_⚠️ Pain Points_**
|-
|Informal P2P sales are time-consuming
|Discord sales feel chaotic
|No escrow or buyer protection
|Hard to build trust at scale
|Managing payments manually

|**_🧩 What They Need From Platform_**
|-
|Simple drop submission flow
|Escrow protection
|Reputation system
|Clear rules (tier, value minimums, shipping window)

<br/><br/>

### 🧑‍💼 The Power Curator (Platform Stabilizer)

Role: Semi-professional curator who sources small bulk lots intentionally, treating this as a side income with the goal of building repeat buyers.

|**_👤 Profile_**
|-  
|Semi-professional
|Sources small bulk lots intentionally
|Treats this as side income
|Wants repeat buyers

|**_🎯 Goals_**
|-
|Predictable drop cadence
|Analytics on sell-through
|Audience building
|Premium tier positioning

|**_⚠️ Pain Points_**
|-
|Unclear demand forecasting
|Limited exposure in fragmented communities
|Manual marketing effort

|**_🧩 What They Need_**
|-
|Drop performance stats
|Email notification tools
|Featured placement option
|Inventory planning signals

<br/><br/>

## Buyers

Buyers are the consumers who purchase drops. They are typically hobbyists or enthusiasts looking for specific parts, limited editions, or curated bundles. They value trust, convenience, and the thrill of discovering unique items.

<br/>

### 🧑‍🎮 The Enthusiast Buyer

|**_👤 Profile_**|
|-  
|Builds, mods, upgrades gear  
|Values discovery and trust
|Already participates in group buys or mystery packs
|Mid-to-high discretionary spend

|**_🎯 Goals_**|
|-
|Discover interesting parts
|Trust that bundle value exceeds price
|Participate in cultural events

|**_⚠️ Pain Points_**|
|-
|Informal P2P transactions
|Risk of low-quality bundles
|Overbrowsing generic marketplaces

|**_🧩 What They Need_**|
|-
|Standardized tier pricing
|Clear value expectations
|Curator ratings
|Escrow confidence
|Drop countdown anticipation
